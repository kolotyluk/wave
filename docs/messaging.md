# Messaging

How Wave is explained, so the story stays the same whoever tells it. Google
Wave was explained by its technology, and too many people never learned what
it was for. Wave leads with the journey.

## Three levels

**The trip, for the public.** One specific journey per audience, in plain
words. "Get help with your housing letter." "Have your say on the bylaw."
"Settle the dispute with your co-op." Never the technology first.

**The vehicle and the network, for decision-makers.** Canada Wave is
infrastructure, like the road network: built once, to common rules, and used
for many journeys. A Wave is the vehicle, and it keeps the record of the
journey.

**The engine, for engineers.** The Side Door, the protocol, the
[architecture](architecture.md) and the [decision records](decisions/README.md).

## The line

> ***Collaboration has always been a journey. Canada Wave keeps the record of it.***

## The metaphor, and its limits

- Canada Wave is the road network; a Wave is the vehicle.
- The Front Door is the driver's seat; the Side Door is under the hood.
- The record is the flight recorder: it exists so that disputes can be settled
  and the system can learn, not to blame.
- Federation is provincial road networks joining: you cross the border
  without changing cars.

For a first reader, use the first line and the flight recorder. The rest is
for those who ask.

## Words

- **Canada Wave**: the infrastructure, the network.
- **a Wave**: one vehicle, one shared journey and its record.
