# Messaging

How Wave is explained, so the story stays the same whoever tells it. Google
Wave was explained by its technology, and too many people never learned what
it was for. Wave leads with the journey.

## Three levels

**The trip, for the public.** One specific journey per audience, in plain
words. "Get help with your housing letter." "Have your say on the bylaw."
"Settle the dispute with your co-op." Never the technology first.

**The vehicle and the network, for decision-makers.** Canada Wave is
infrastructure, like the road network: built once, to common rules, and used
for many journeys. A Wave is the vehicle, and it keeps the record of the
journey.

**The engine, for engineers.** The Side Door, the protocol, the
[architecture](architecture.md) and the [decision records](decisions/README.md).

## The line

> ***Collaboration has always been a journey. Canada Wave keeps the record of it.***

## The metaphor, and its limits

- Canada Wave is the road network; a Wave is the vehicle.
- The Front Door is the driver's seat; the Side Door is under the hood.
- The record is the flight recorder: it exists so that disputes can be settled
  and the system can learn, not to blame.
- Federation is provincial road networks joining: you cross the border
  without changing cars.

For a first reader, use the first line and the flight recorder. The rest is
for those who ask.

## Surfing, the companion image

The road and the flight recorder are for decision-makers: they explain the
infrastructure and the record. Surfing is for everyone else: it explains what
working together in a Wave feels like, and the name already says it.

> ***Many boards, one wave.***

- **The wave** is the shared object: one wave, everyone on it, always moving.
- **The boards** are the doors. Bring your own (the default, a legal-aid
  agency's, anyone's) and ride the same Wave as everyone else.
- **Two on a board** is a shared door: a support worker beside someone, a
  family on one phone, a lawyer and client at one screen.
- **Body surfing** is the telephone door: riding the wave with no equipment
  at all.
- **Dolphins** are the robots: not people, plainly not people, and entirely at
  home on the wave.
- **Surf etiquette** is the protocol: right of way, and nobody drops in on
  someone else's wave.

The two images settle each other. Surfing is the moment of working together,
live and moving. The road and the recorder are what lasts.

## Have fun

Surfers are inventive and playful, and so is Wave, however serious its work.
Look for chances to have fun: in names (Prissy, Gort), in documentation, in
the community around it.

Fun never comes at the expense of a person in difficulty. Someone facing a
housing crisis or a court gets warmth and clarity, not jokes.

## Words

- **Canada Wave**: the infrastructure, the network.
- **a Wave**: one vehicle, one shared journey and its record.
- **Ztuph Wave**: the development and demonstration instance, at `ztuph.com`.
